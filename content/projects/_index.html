Methods, metascience, and small experiments — tools and habits for more honest research.

01 / methods & tools

[ 01 ]

MCPower — Monte Carlo Power Analysis

Monte Carlo power analysis for complex models. Find the sample size you need, or check whether a study has enough power, even where traditional analytic formulas can’t reach. Available as a Python package and a standalone desktop app.

[ 02 ]

Likelihood Ratio Test for Publication Bias

A statistical method designed to detect and quantify publication bias in datasets of heterogeneous study results.

in development
[ 03 ]

PLSKit

Cross-language partial least squares with a modern inference layer — Rust core, Python / R / Julia wrappers.

02 / open science

[ 01 ]

Society for Open Science

Founder and board member. Promoting collaboration, transparency, and the practical reuse of research results — by scientists, educators, NGOs, industry, and the wider public. A transparent, critical methodology that makes work replicable and verifiable.

[ 02 ]

CATS Conference

An interdisciplinary metascience conference where discussion and individual feedback come first — bringing all academic levels into one room to wrestle with the real challenges of doing research.

[ 03 ]

Estimating Replicability of Polish Psychology

A study on how higher-education reform and the Publish-or-Perish dynamic shape questionable research practices, with a comparison and ranking of Polish psychology institutes by publication-bias indicators.

03 / programming

Procrustes

Orthogonal Procrustes and signed-permutation matrix alignment — a small, fast Rust crate built on faer.

in development
[ 02 ]

Inough

An R package for detecting inattention in long psychophysical tasks — flagging trials where participants have likely disengaged.

in development
[ 03 ]

Leyline

Mapping the hidden connections between studies, datasets, and authors — a graph layer over the literature.

04 / other projects

[ 01 ]

ReproducibiliTea — Warsaw

A series of seminar meetings in the ReproducibiliTea format, introducing Open Science to students and the curious public — and naming the problems that the movement, in Poland and beyond, is trying to address.

[ 02 ]

Methodological School Under Sails

A summer school on the Mazurian Lakes pairing philosophy-of-science and statistics workshops with sailing — run annually with the Society for Open Science.
